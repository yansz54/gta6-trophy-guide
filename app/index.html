    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving. Everything except the one flagged meditation can be finished after the story in free-roam.

    1 potentially missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation when it appears (see Stage 2).

    The 100% checklist (A Long Road)

    Visible in-game only after the story (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion), but progress made during the story already counts. The list is forgiving — closer to 60% true completion.
