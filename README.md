Leonida Legend — the complete GTA VI trophy guide & tracker: all 55 trophies with official names, descriptions and grades, own-words cited guides, a localStorage tracker with grade/kind/earned filters and a spoiler mode, export/import of progress, and a staged roadmap to the platinum. Works fully offline.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. No network requests — the app only loads its own files.

## What it does

- **All 55 trophies** with the official name, description and grade exactly as PlayStation publishes them, plus an own-words guide with steps and cited sources (PowerPyx and Exophase) for every trophy.
- **Tracker**: check off trophies as you earn them; progress is saved in `localStorage` with a progress bar and per-grade counts, and can be exported to a JSON file and imported back.
- **Filters**: by grade (platinum/gold/silver/bronze), by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities), by earned/not-earned, plus text search across name, description and guide.
- **Spoiler mode** (on by default): hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until they are earned.
- **Roadmap to the platinum**: stages 0–6 in order, the 100% checklist for A Long Road, and a clear flag on the one potentially missable trophy — Positive Mental Attitude.
- **Honest unknowns**: 5 trophies (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) have no confirmed method as of 19 Nov 2026 — the guide says so and gives only the best unverified lead, never a guessed method as fact.

## Facts

55 trophies · 1 platinum / 2 gold / 6 silver / 46 bronze · 28 hidden on PlayStation · difficulty 4/10 · ~200 hours to platinum · 55 offline, 0 online · 1 playthrough · no mission replay · no New Game+ · 1 potentially missable trophy.
